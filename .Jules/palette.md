## 2025-05-14 - Standardizing Accessibility in AssetTrack Dashboards
**Learning:** Dashboard prototypes often lack foundational keyboard accessibility such as skip-to-content links and visible focus indicators for icon-only buttons.
**Action:** Implement 'Skip to Main Content' as the first child of <body> and apply standardized `focus-visible:ring-2` styles to all interactive elements to ensure clear visual feedback during keyboard navigation.
