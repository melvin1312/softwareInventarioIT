# Palette's Journal - AssetTrack UX Improvements

## 2025-05-15 - Standardized Login Accessibility & Interaction
**Learning:** Login pages often neglect accessibility in favor of "security" aesthetics. Combining high-contrast focus rings with functional interactive elements (like a working password toggle) significantly improves usability for all users without compromising the "secure" feel.
**Action:** Always implement functional password toggles (not just icons) and provide skip-to-content links even on relatively simple pages like login to maintain a consistent accessibility baseline across the platform.
