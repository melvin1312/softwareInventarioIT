## 2023-10-27 - Password Visibility Toggle & State Synchronization
**Learning:** In static HTML login prototypes, adding custom JS submission transitions can easily break natural form submission/redirection and bloat the code beyond strict change-size constraints (e.g. 50 lines). Instead, focus on clean, regression-free accessibility upgrades. When implementing password unmasking, you must dynamically synchronize the `aria-pressed` state, update the descriptive `aria-label` and `title` tags, and modify both the visual icon's textContent (for material icons) and its `data-icon` attribute to ensure perfect alignment across assistive technologies and rendering engines.
**Action:** Keep password visibility toggles minimal (< 15 lines of JS), focus strictly on accessibility enhancements, and avoid blocking natural form submissions.
